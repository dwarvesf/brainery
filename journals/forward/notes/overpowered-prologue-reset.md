## The finding

Open with the player already at full power, then make losing that power the event that starts progression. Vincent surfaced the idea while the build-club thread was discussing a game's visual update: the player begins as a celestial figure with complete equipment and magic, causes havoc, and is cast down to restart as an ordinary level-one character. The proposal is captured in the [Discord thread](https://discord.com/channels/462663954813157376/1280726623414390805/1560530413175312536).

## Why it matters to us

This structure gives a familiar level-one reset an explicit narrative cause. It can also make the opening teach the destination before the player enters the normal progression loop: the prologue shows the fantasy at full strength, while the reset establishes what must be rebuilt. For the game being explored in build-club, that is a concrete onboarding direction rather than extra lore detached from play.

## Take it further

- Prototype a short playable prologue using the complete loadout, then reuse the same encounter after the reset to show the contrast.
- Decide which power, item, or visual effect survives the fall so the player keeps one thread connecting the two states.
- Compare a playable fall with a cutscene-only version and observe which one makes the reset clearer without making the opening drag.
- Define what evidence would reject the idea, such as players reading the reset as punishment rather than the start of progression.
