## Finding

zlatanpham suggested keeping a new game in TestFlight longer, inviting more people to test it, and submitting the iOS version before the Android release. The practical concern was sequencing: shipping Android first can leave a cross-platform launch waiting on an iOS rejection or resubmission.

## Why it matters

Store review is part of the delivery path, not an after-release chore. Front-loading iOS review and using TestFlight for real play can expose approval and product issues while the release plan still has room to move.
