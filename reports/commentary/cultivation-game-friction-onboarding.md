## The exchange

bievh dropped 9T into #build-club late on 1 October. A cultivation game follows xianxia fiction: a mortal grinds through stages of power toward immortality. tieubao's first request was a cheat code to skip to a sword technique.

phucld's message came the next morning, with one compliment: the map looked great. bievh's reply: "còn nhiều cơ chế ẩn ai mê thì tự mò, ko thì bye" (there are many hidden mechanics, fans dig for them, otherwise bye). The guide had already been cut: "cái guide 15 tab em rút còn 4 tab thì phải" (the 15-tab guide, I cut to 4, I think).

## The loop the guide shows

bievh stated the first loop in one message: "tu lên lvl 1 => đánh quái => bán đồ => mua gạo => tu => sau lvl 3 thì cần linh thạch để tu" (cultivate to level 1, fight monsters, sell the drops, buy rice, cultivate; after level 3 you need spirit stones). Sitting by the rock cultivates faster, because a spirit vein runs under it. NPCs show the player how to play. There are no quests, so the player does what they like.

## What stays hidden

On release night bievh wrote: "co nhieu easter egg tu tien trong design lam, ma choi tu tu moi vui, hack ti het vui" (many cultivation easter eggs are in the design; play slowly and it is fun; hack and the fun is gone). His examples:

- Apprentice alchemy burns firewood, so the fire is "phàm hỏa", mundane fire. The game does not say so.
- Spiritual roots are random, each with its own aura. A weak root means a longer game.
- Once the character outlevels a monster, the monster runs instead of fighting.
- huymaius asked for a pit with a secret manual at the bottom. bievh said he is designing "bí cảnh", secret realms.

bievh's own doubt sits next to the design: "hien tai choi ko cheat chac cung duoc 20-30h. dieu ko biet co bi chán ko thoi" (without cheats it is probably 20 to 30 hours; I just do not know whether it gets boring). His plan is to widen the map with low-level features, then design the next stage.

The genre has a precedent for shipping without hand-holding: PCGamesN reported that Tale of Immortal, a Chinese cultivation RPG that entered Early Access in January 2021, [sold nearly two million copies in its first month](https://www.pcgamesn.com/tale-of-immortal/sales).

## The prologue idea

Two weeks later, under a graphics update, tuanddd proposed the other half of the onboarding: "làm story kiểu mình là đại thần tiên trên trời, đầu game nhảy vô là full trang bị + phép, đi tung hoành tùm lum xong bị đày xuống trùng sinh thành người thường (reset lv1)" (make the story so you are a great immortal in the heavens; you start with full gear and spells, wreak havoc, then get banished and reborn as a mortal, reset to level 1). bievh answered "legit" and said he would try it.

The prologue shows the fantasy at full strength before the rice-and-rock grind, and gives the level-1 start a cause inside the fiction. The thread did not settle whether the opening is playable or a cutscene.
